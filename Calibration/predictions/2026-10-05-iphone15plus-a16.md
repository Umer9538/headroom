# Pre-registered prediction: iPhone 15 Plus (A16 Bionic)

Written and pushed **before any language model was run on this phone**. The A16
has never been part of Headroom's calibration, which is a single device (the M1),
so this is an out-of-sample test.

**Device:** iPhone 15 Plus (`iPhone15,5`), A16 Bionic, 6 GB, iOS 27.0 (24A437).

**What is predicted:** TinyLlama-1.1B Q4_0 single-stream decode with
llama.cpp-Metal at `95ef7fc`, as the PocketRoofline app measures it.

## The rule, fixed now

1. The phone is unplugged, in airplane mode, and has cooled to thermal state
   `nominal`. The Headroom demo's probe is then run three times.
2. Of those three reports, the one whose GPU triad median is the middle value is
   used.
3. **Peak prediction:** `estimate(.tinyLlama1_1BQ4_0, contextTokens: 128).peak`,
   as printed by `headroom-probe --from <report>`. The estimator and
   `calibration.json` are the ones last changed in commit `d41bbd1`, and they are
   not edited before the comparison is published.
4. **Measured value:** the SISO decode mean from the PocketRoofline app's
   matrix v1 run (128 tokens decoded from an empty cache, 5 repeats after one
   warmup), started from the same cold state straight after the probes.
5. **Secondary:** the sustained interval at 1,024 tokens against the decode rate
   of the last SILO repeat. That interval comes from two other phones on two
   runtimes, so it is a range check rather than a device prediction.
6. The comparison is published whatever it shows. If the measured value falls
   outside the interval, that is reported as a miss.

## Pilot probes: retained, but not the prediction

Three probes were taken first, while the phone was charging over USB and at
thermal state `serious` after a restart. They do not meet the conditions above,
and they are kept in [`../runs/pilot/`](../runs/pilot/) because no run is
discarded.

| Captured (UTC) | Thermal | Power | GPU triad median | CPU triad median (2 threads) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-05 01:01:46 | serious | external | 38.52 GB/s | 48.21 GB/s |
| 2026-10-05 01:01:52 | serious | external | 44.12 GB/s | 47.48 GB/s |
| 2026-10-05 01:01:57 | serious | external | 45.31 GB/s | 46.70 GB/s |

Applying the rule to the pilot reports would give a peak interval of
46.6–48.9 tok/s, from the middle probe at 44.12 GB/s. That is not the
pre-registered prediction, because the conditions differ.

## Result (added 2026-10-05, after the run)

**The peak prediction missed.** The rule above was not edited.

*Correction, same day:* this section first gave the measured mean as 65.17 tok/s and the sustained miss as 0.5%. The exact mean of the five repeats is 65.1648 (65.16 to two decimals; 65.17 came from rounding twice), and the exact sustained miss is 0.45%. Neither outcome changes, and the +32.1% figure is the same either way.

| | Predicted (rule applied) | Measured | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peak decode (SISO) | 48.15–49.34 tok/s | **65.16 tok/s** (mean of 5, sd 0.52) | **Miss**: 32.1% above the upper bound |
| Sustained (last SILO repeat) | 27.29–34.57 tok/s | 27.16 tok/s | Miss: 0.45% below the lower bound |

### Protocol deviations

Both deviations are recorded, and neither changes the outcome.

- **The phone was not cooled to `nominal`.**
  - The probes were taken 3.5 minutes after the pilot, at thermal state `fair`, and the benchmark also started at `fair`.
  - The phone was unplugged (battery 60%), in airplane mode, with Low Power Mode off.
  - A cooler start would be expected to raise the measured value, which would widen the miss.
- **Four probes were taken, not three.**
  - The rule was applied to the first three. Their GPU triad medians were 45.89, 45.31 and 45.05 GB/s, so the 45.31 probe was used.
  - Every one of the four probes predicts 47.4–50.1 tok/s, so any choice misses by about the same amount.

### Why it missed

The estimate assumes that single-stream decode reaches the same fraction η of the measured STREAM ceiling on every device. η came from a single device.

| Device | Decode bandwidth (tok/s × 0.636 GB) | Measured GPU triad ceiling | η |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 (calibration) | 38.86 GB/s | 56.00 GB/s | 0.694 |
| A16 (this run) | 41.44 GB/s | 45.31 GB/s | **0.915** |

One laptop does not calibrate a phone.

### What changes

- The A16 joins the calibration, which brings it to n = 2. The interval widens to show how little is known about η yet.
- This phone can no longer serve as a validation. The next out-of-sample test is the next phone.

### Not yet explained

SILO decode fell from 59.17 tok/s (the last LISO repeat) to 29.69 tok/s (the first SILO repeat) within seconds. Over the same step, prefill *rose* from 429 to 465 tok/s.

A plain thermal slowdown would have pulled prefill down too, so the "sustained" figure may be a step in the memory or power state rather than a gradual decline. A cold run with SILO first is needed before that number is quoted anywhere.
