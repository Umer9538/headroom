# Pre-registered prediction: iPhone 15 Plus (A16 Bionic)

Written and pushed **before any language model was run on this phone**. The A16
has never been part of Headroom's calibration, which is a single device (the M1),
so this is an out-of-sample test.

**Device:** iPhone 15 Plus (`iPhone15,5`), A16 Bionic, 6 GB, iOS 27.0 (24A437).

**What is predicted:** TinyLlama-1.1B Q4_0 single-stream decode with
llama.cpp-Metal at `95ef7fc`, as the PocketRoofline app measures it.

## The rule, fixed now

1. The phone is unplugged, in airplane mode, and has cooled to thermal state
   `nominal`. The Headroom demo's probe is then run three times.
2. Of those three reports, the one whose GPU triad median is the middle value is
   used.
3. **Peak prediction:** `estimate(.tinyLlama1_1BQ4_0, contextTokens: 128).peak`,
   as printed by `headroom-probe --from <report>`. The estimator and
   `calibration.json` are the ones last changed in commit `d41bbd1`, and they are
   not edited before the comparison is published.
4. **Measured value:** the SISO decode mean from the PocketRoofline app's
   matrix v1 run (128 tokens decoded from an empty cache, 5 repeats after one
   warmup), started from the same cold state straight after the probes.
5. **Secondary:** the sustained interval at 1,024 tokens against the decode rate
   of the last SILO repeat. That interval comes from two other phones on two
   runtimes, so it is a range check rather than a device prediction.
6. The comparison is published whatever it shows. If the measured value falls
   outside the interval, that is reported as a miss.

## Pilot probes: retained, but not the prediction

Three probes were taken first, while the phone was charging over USB and at
thermal state `serious` after a restart. They do not meet the conditions above,
and they are kept in [`../runs/pilot/`](../runs/pilot/) because no run is
discarded.

| Captured (UTC) | Thermal | Power | GPU triad median | CPU triad median (2 threads) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-05 01:01:46 | serious | external | 38.52 GB/s | 48.21 GB/s |
| 2026-10-05 01:01:52 | serious | external | 44.12 GB/s | 47.48 GB/s |
| 2026-10-05 01:01:57 | serious | external | 45.31 GB/s | 46.70 GB/s |

Applying the rule to the pilot reports would give a peak interval of
46.6–48.9 tok/s, from the middle probe at 44.12 GB/s. That is not the
pre-registered prediction, because the conditions differ.
